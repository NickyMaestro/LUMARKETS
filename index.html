📄 Terms & Regulations

1. About LUMARKETS

LUMARKETS connects farmers directly to buyers. We do not buy or sell produce ourselves. We provide the platform for you to meet.

2. For Sellers (Farmers)

  • Only post produce you actually have.
  • Give honest price, quantity and location.
  • Do not post stolen or expired goods.
  • Respond to buyers quickly and honestly.

3. For Buyers

  • Confirm price, quantity and delivery before paying.
  • Inspect produce before paying where possible.
  • Pay the seller directly. LUMARKETS does not hold your money.

4. Payments & Delivery

Buyer and seller agree on payment (Cash, M-Pesa, etc.) and delivery. LUMARKETS is not responsible for delivery delays or quality disputes, but we will help resolve them.

5. Prohibited Items

No illegal goods, no fake products, no fraud. Accounts that cheat will be removed.

6. Privacy

Your name and phone are only used so buyers and sellers can contact each other. Do not share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone.

7. Agreement

By using LUMARKETS, you agree to these terms and to trade fairly and respectfully.
